The harbour lamps come on at five,
and gulls go quiet on the pier.

A tram bell rings on Hafenstraße,
the last one, late, for once, this year.
	The baker counts his loaves by hand,
the ferry coughs and leaves the quay;
someone is singing, badly, in French,
and someone else is making tea.

By six the rain has made its mind up.
By seven nobody remembers why.
